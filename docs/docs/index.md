# Keeping a Home Country Business Compliant While You Work Abroad

*By Nomad Desk Editorial*

Freelancers and small business owners who move onto a [digital nomad visa](https://nomadbriefing.com/digital-nomad-visa/japan/) often keep their home country LLC, sole proprietorship, or limited company running exactly as it was — same registered address, same accountant, same annual filings. That's usually fine for a few months. Left unattended for a year or two of long-stay travel, it quietly stops being fine.

## What tends to lapse first

- **Registered agent or business address.** Many jurisdictions require a business to maintain a valid registered address for legal notices — a friend's house you moved out of two years ago doesn't count, and missed notices can lead to administrative dissolution without you knowing.
- **Annual report filings.** Most company registries require a simple annual report or franchise tax filing to stay in good standing. It's easy to forget when you're managing life across a [Portugal](https://nomadbriefing.com/portugal/) or [Thailand](https://nomadbriefing.com/thailand/) stay and the reminder email goes to an inbox you don't check daily.
- **Where the business is actually managed from.** Some countries determine corporate tax residency based on where key management decisions are made, not just where the company is registered — running a business day-to-day from abroad for an extended period can, in some cases, create tax obligations in the country you're now based in.

> This isn't legal or tax advice — a local accountant familiar with cross-border small business rules can confirm what actually applies to your specific structure and destination, including if that destination shifts to [Mexico](https://nomadbriefing.com/mexico/) partway through the year.

## A short maintenance checklist

- Confirm the registered address is current and someone reliable checks mail sent there
- Calendar annual report and franchise tax deadlines well ahead, not when a lapse notice arrives
- Ask an accountant whether extended management from abroad changes anything about corporate tax residency
- Keep basic admin — banking, invoicing currency, [connectivity for calls with the accountant](https://nomadbriefing.com/esim/) — as simple and low-maintenance as possible while traveling
